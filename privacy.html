Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Purpose

This project is a personal integration that uses rclone and the Google Drive API to access the owner's Google Drive from a personally operated Raspberry Pi.

Information accessed

With the owner's authorization, rclone can access Google Drive files and folders within the permissions granted to it.

How information is used

Google Drive information is accessed solely to enable the owner to manage and access their own files. This website does not provide a Google Drive service to other users.

Storage and sharing

Files accessed through rclone may be stored or cached on the owner's Raspberry Pi in accordance with the local rclone configuration. OAuth configuration and tokens are maintained locally by rclone. Google Drive information is not intentionally shared with third parties through this website.

Website data

This informational website does not provide a login form, request Google Drive files, or intentionally collect personal information through its pages. GitHub may process technical information as part of hosting this website under its own policies.

Contact

For questions about this personal project, contact the owner through their GitHub profile.
